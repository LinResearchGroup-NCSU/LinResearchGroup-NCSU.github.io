The Lin group is grateful to receive a new [NSF award](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award?AWD_ID=2609743), "Integrating Sequence, Structure, and Dynamics for Predicting and Designing Nucleic Acids," supporting our work on biophysics-informed AI models for protein-nucleic acid prediction.

This project, carried out in close collaboration with [Prof. Keith Weninger](https://physics.sciences.ncsu.edu/people/krwening/) and [Prof. Yi Xiao](https://chemistry.sciences.ncsu.edu/people/yi-xiao/), will develop a data-efficient computational platform for predicting protein-DNA and protein-RNA binding affinities and designing high-affinity oligonucleotide aptamers.

We are excited about the scientific discoveries ahead!
