Lin Group Receives NSF Award for Nucleic Acid Design

July 10, 2026

The Lin group is grateful to receive a new NSF award, "Integrating Sequence, Structure, and Dynamics for Predicting and Designing Nucleic Acids," to support our work on physics-informed AI models for protein-nucleic acid recognition.

This collaborative project, with Keith Weninger and Yi Xiao as co-PIs, will develop data-efficient computational tools for predicting protein-DNA and protein-RNA binding affinities and for designing high-affinity nucleic acid motifs.

We are excited about the scientific discoveries, software development, and educational opportunities that lie ahead!
