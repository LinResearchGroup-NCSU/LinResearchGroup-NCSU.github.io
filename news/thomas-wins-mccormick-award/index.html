Thomas Wins McCormick Award

May 14, 2026

Congratulations to our graduating undergraduate researcher, Thomas Thornton, on receiving the 2026 McCormick Award, the Department of Physics and Astronomy’s highest honor for undergraduate research!

Thomas presented his award talk, titled “Structure-Based Modeling of RNA and Biomolecular Condensates,” during the award ceremony.
