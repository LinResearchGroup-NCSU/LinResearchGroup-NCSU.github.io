Postdoctoral Fellows

We are looking for a postdoctoral fellow who is proficient in computational simulations of biomolecules. If you are interested, please send a CV, research interests, and the names and contact information of three professional references to Xingcheng_Lin@ncsu.edu.

Graduate Students

We always welcome motivated graduate students from various backgrounds to join our group. If you are interested in our research, don’t hesitate to email Xingcheng_Lin@ncsu.edu to set up a meeting.

Undergraduate Students

Our group warmly welcomes undergraduate students interested in exploring computational research. Feel free to reach out to Xingcheng_Lin@ncsu.edu to start a discussion.
